# Cafe Sales Analysis (Excel Project)

A practice data analysis project on 3 months of cafe orders (July to September 2026), done in Microsoft Excel. The data is synthetic (made up for learning), not from a real cafe.

## What I did

- Worked with 5,211 order rows: date, time, item, category, quantity, price and cost.
- Added calculated columns:
  - Revenue = Quantity x Price
  - Item Margin = Quantity x (Price - Cost)
  - Month and Weekday from the order date
- Built Pivot Tables for sales by item, by month and by category.
- Added charts: revenue by item and revenue by month.

## Key results

- Total revenue for 3 months: Rs. 930,590
- Top 3 items by revenue: Cold Coffee Rs. 194,460, Cappuccino Rs. 158,520, Veg Sandwich Rs. 111,760
- Revenue by month: July Rs. 329,290, August Rs. 312,000, September Rs. 289,300
- Category share: Beverages 53.45%, Food 34.47%, Desserts 12.08%

## What I found

- More than half of the cafe's revenue comes from beverages.
- Cold Coffee brings in the highest revenue of any single item.
- Sales fell month by month, about 12% lower in September than in July.

## What I would recommend

- Keep the focus on beverages, since they drive most of the revenue.
- Try food and drink combos to grow the food category.
- Watch the next months' numbers to see if the drop continues before making bigger changes.
